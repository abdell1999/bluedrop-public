How it works

Three steps. Zero effort.

Everything happens in the background, automatically.

01

You leave your car

Your car's Bluetooth disconnects. BlueDrop catches that exact moment without you doing anything.

02

Location is saved

GPS coordinates and street address are captured instantly. Stored only on your device — no BlueDrop servers, no account.

03

Navigate back

Open BlueDrop or tap the widget, and Google Maps gives you walking directions back to your car.

Features

Simple by design.

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Fully automatic

No button to press. Bluetooth disconnect is the trigger. BlueDrop handles the rest.

100% private

All location data lives on your phone. No cloud sync, no analytics, no third-party trackers.

Full history

Browse every past parking spot with address, date, time, and an integrated map view.

Works offline

Location saving requires no internet connection. Maps load from OpenStreetMap when online.

Notes & photos

Attach a note or photo to any record — handy for parking garage levels or bay numbers.

Instant notification

A notification showing your parking address appears the moment you step out of the car.
